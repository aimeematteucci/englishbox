Step 3 · Your rule
can + verbI can swim.
Can you drive?Most abilities
know + informationI know the answer.
Do you know where it is?Facts, people, places
know how to + verbShe knows how to fix computers.The steps, the method

Never: I know swim. I know to swim.

@aimee.matteucci
English Box
What real English says

For abilities, can wins.

In large collections of English texts, people say I can swim much more often than I know how to swim.

Know how to = you learned the steps. “She knows how to change a tire.”
